# Research: what Cluck Life can learn from farm, pet, and life sims

This note compares **Cluck Life** (a phone-first raise-and-fight chicken game) with the games people actually play in the same neighborhood: Roblox farm sims, pet-care games, life sims, plus the original **Duck Life** loop this project already follows.

Nothing here is copied. No Roblox or Duck Life code, models, audio, or art is reused. The list below is **feature ideas only** — systems and feelings we can rebuild in our own world.

## What Cluck Life already is

A single chicken, trained with short tap-timing sessions (Peck / Run / Dodge), fed or napped for energy, then sent up an 8-rival fight ladder with coin bets. Speed decides who pecks first and how often someone dodges. After King Comb, champion tiers bring the same rivals back stronger. Progress lives in the browser. There is no shop and no passcode.

The gap versus the genre hits: the farm never felt like a place, care was two buttons, and the chicken was a flat drawing.

## Comparable games

### Duck Life (Wix Games) — the original loop

The series (2007 onward) is “train a bird in mini-games, then prove it in a contest.” Early games race; **Duck Life: Battle** swaps the contest for fights. You feed for energy, train one stat at a time, climb a ladder of harder events, and spend coins on **cosmetic** colors and hats that do not change power.

- Official / developer: [Duck Life 1 on Wix Games](https://games.wixgames.co.uk/game.php?id=ducklife1), [Wix Games DuckLife](http://www.wixgames.co.uk/game.asp?id=9)
- Series overview: [Duck Life (series) Fandom](https://ducklife.fandom.com/wiki/Duck_Life_(series)), [TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/DuckLife)
- Battle spin: [Duck Life: Battle on CrazyGames](https://www.crazygames.com/game/duck-life-battle)
- Playable recap: [ducklife.online](https://ducklife.online/)

**Borrow:** keep the train → feed → contest loop; add a small cosmetic look picker (colors only, no paywall); keep training as a visible station in the world.

**Leave alone:** paid shops, hat catalogs, racing tracks, and any 2D sprite style from those games.

### Grow a Garden (Roblox) — the farm hit

The 2025–2026 Roblox breakout. Plant on a personal plot, wait (including offline), harvest, sell. Crops have stages; later updates added weather, mutations, and helper pets. The official pitch is cozy and free-to-play: “your garden literally grows while you are offline.” Mobile-friendly, low combat pressure.

- Official: [Grow a Garden on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/126884695634066/Grow-a-Garden)
- Context: [Roblox farming games 2026 (Lootbar)](https://www.lootbar.com/blog/en/roblox-farming-games-2026-from-grow-a-garden-to-bee-swarm.html), [Games like Grow a Garden (The Spike)](https://www.thespike.gg/roblox/best-roblox-games/best-games-like-grow-a-garden), [Grow a Garden codes / how it plays (The Spike)](https://www.thespike.gg/roblox/game-codes/grow-a-garden-roblox-codes)

Nearby farm titles with the same “plot you tend” fantasy: **Farming and Friends**, **Garden Horizons**, **Egg Case Farm** (chickens + eggs), **Bee Swarm Simulator** (collection + a place that feels busy).

**Egg Case Farm** (and the “something waiting in the coop” beat from other chicken games) is the source for Cluck Life’s nest: the coop slowly lays **one** coin egg. Collect it, then wait again. No pile-up, no offline tycoon.

**Borrow:** three real corn plots in the yard; plant with coins; grow on a timer (works while the tab is closed); harvest for coins and a sip of energy. Show empty / sprout / ready in 3D.

**Leave alone:** mutations, trading, steal-at-night PvP, Robux shops, offline tycoon automation.

### Adopt Me! (Uplift Games) — pet care as a bond

Roblox’s long-running pet-care / roleplay game. Pets hatch, then grow through ages if you meet needs (hungry, sleepy, thirsty, bored…). Care earns the in-game currency. Homes and looks are part of the fantasy. The care loop is the attachment, not a DPS number.

- Official: [Adopt Me! on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/920587237/Adopt-Me)
- Overview: [Wikipedia: Adopt Me!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adopt_Me!), [Roblox Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Uplift_Games/Adopt_Me!)
- Compare-and-contrast with number-go-up pets: [Pet Simulator 99 vs Adopt Me (2026)](https://earnaldo.com/blog/pet-simulator-99-vs-adopt-me)

**Borrow:** feed and nap should *look* like care (walk to the trough, doze by the barn); the chicken should idle, wander, blink, and react. Cosmetic looks you pick yourself. A named bird you can rename.

**Leave alone:** aging stages, neon fusion, trading, houses, and any paid eggs.

### Pet Simulator 99 (BIG Games) — what not to copy

Hatch eggs, bigger numbers, auto-farm, area gates. Pets are power, not companions. Fine as a warning: Cluck Life should stay a *one bird you raise*, not a loot treadmill.

- [Pet Simulator 99 Fandom](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Pet_Simulator_99)
- [Roblox Fandom page](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/BIG_Games_Pets/Pet_Simulator_99)

**Borrow:** only the “this fight is a show” beat — a short, readable payoff when you land a crit. No eggs, no auto-hatch.

### Welcome to Bloxburg — life with a rhythm

Jobs earn money; hunger / energy / fun / hygiene meters shape the day; you spend on a home. Progression is “work, restore, improve the place.”

- Compare: [Bloxburg vs Brookhaven (2026)](https://earnaldo.com/blog/welcome-to-bloxburg-vs-brookhaven-rp)
- Genre roundup: [Roblox simulators / life sims](https://gamevector.blog/roblox-simulators-best-life-sim-games/)

**Borrow:** energy as a real day-rhythm (already in Cluck Life); the yard should feel like *your* place — barn, coop, fence, feeder, garden — not a blank stage.

**Leave alone:** job boards, multi-meter chores, and build-mode furniture catalogs.

### Brookhaven RP — a place you want to stand in

Roblox’s highest-visit life hangout: a suburban town, claim a house, wander. Almost no grind. The lesson is atmosphere and immediate access — you are in the world in seconds, no gate.

- [Roblox Fandom: Brookhaven RP](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Brookhaven_by_Voldex/Brookhaven_RP)
- [Brookhaven parent/overview](https://screenwiseapp.com/guides/brookhaven-roblox)

**Borrow:** load straight into a cozy yard (no passcode, no tutorial lock). Soft lighting, a readable landmark (the red barn), and a camera that can glance around.

**Leave alone:** open roleplay, vehicles, other players.

### Training-zone sims (Gym League and friends)

Roblox “go stand on the pad to train” games make the *place* teach the verb. Lists of Grow a Garden-likes often include **Gym League** as the training counterpart ([The Spike](https://www.thespike.gg/roblox/best-roblox-games/best-games-like-grow-a-garden)).

**Borrow:** a peck dummy, a dirt track, and dodge posts in the yard. Tapping them starts the same training as the buttons.

## Features we are borrowing (and how)

| Source | Idea | How it lands in Cluck Life |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Duck Life | Train one stat, then a contest | Already the core. Keep it. Training is now a 3D station + the same tap bar. |
| Duck Life | Cosmetic colors, not power | Free look picker on the Chicken tab. Looks do not change stats. |
| Duck Life: Battle | Contest can be a brawl | Already true. Fights now play in a 3D ring with the same beat log. |
| Grow a Garden | Personal plots that grow while away | Three corn plots. Plant 8 coins, ready in 45s, harvest 18 coins + 15 energy. |
| Egg Case Farm | A coop that produces something you collect | One coin egg every 60s. Cap of one waiting egg. Click the nest. |
| Adopt Me | Care is visible | Feed walks to the trough; nap leans by the barn; low energy looks sleepy / hungry (animation only). |
| Adopt Me | Name and appearance | Rename stays. Looks are saved with the chicken. |
| Bloxburg | The home is the progress | Barn, coop, fence, feeder, garden, and a champion banner on the barn as you climb. |
| Brookhaven | Zero-friction start | Game opens on the yard. No passcode, no account, no shop. |
| Training pads | World teaches verbs | Dummy / track / posts are clickable. |
| Pet Sim 99 | Celebrate a rare hit | Crit flashes and particles — not a loot box. |

## What we are not borrowing

- Robux, game passes, or any real-money shop
- Trading, trust-trades, or player markets
- Paywalled houses, eggs, or seeds
- Auto-hatch / AFK number grind
- Crop stealing or PvP
- A passcode or preview gate (the game stays open)
- Anyone else’s models, animations, audio, or UI chrome

## Visual direction (ours)

A **cozy low-poly** farm: warm hemisphere light, a red barn, cream chicken, pastel grass, cheap fake shadows. Built with Three.js from primitive meshes we author ourselves so phones stay smooth: shared geometries, instanced fence/grass/flowers, no GLTF downloads, capped pixel ratio, shadows off on small screens, and a closed-form win-odds estimate instead of 150 live fight simulations on every UI refresh.
